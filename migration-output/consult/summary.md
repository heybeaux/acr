# consult

Multi-model expert panel consultation. Use when: (1) stuck on a problem for 20+ minutes, (2) architecture or pricing decisions with irreversible consequences, (3) user says /consult, (4) genuine blind spot identified. Spawns parallel sub-agents across different models (Gemini, GPT, etc.), collects independent perspectives, and synthesizes into a recommendation. NOT for: routine tasks, obvious answers, or time-critical situations where speed > thoroughness.
